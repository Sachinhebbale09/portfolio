Why wedding photographers need a website, not just Instagram

By Sachin Hebbale · 18 November 2026

Quick answerInstagram is great for showing new work, but it is hard for a couple to quickly see your packages, your best albums and how to book you. A website puts all of that in one place, shows up on Google when people search "wedding photographer near me", and makes you look more established.

1. Couples search on Google, not only on Instagram

When a family starts planning a wedding, many people simply search "wedding photographer in Belagavi" or "pre-wedding shoot Hubballi". A website with your city and services written clearly can appear in those results. An Instagram profile usually won't.

2. Your best work gets lost in the feed

On Instagram, your best wedding from last year is buried under recent posts and reels. A website lets you show hand-picked albums, sorted into weddings, pre-weddings, maternity and baby shoots, so a couple sees your best work in seconds.

3. It answers questions before they call

A simple page with your packages, what's included, the places you travel to, and a "Book a date" button saves you from answering the same questions in DMs again and again.

4. It makes you look established

For a big event, families want to trust the photographer. Your own website, with your name, years of experience and real reviews, builds that trust before the first meeting.

5. You own it

Instagram's algorithm decides who sees your posts. Your website is yours: no algorithm, no follower count, just your work and your contact button.

What a photographer's website needs

  • A strong first screen with your best photo and a "Book your date" button
  • Galleries by category, with fast-loading images
  • Packages or "starting from" information
  • WhatsApp and call buttons on every screen
  • Your Instagram linked, so both work together
