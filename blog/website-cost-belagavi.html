How much does a website cost for a small business in Belagavi?

By Sachin Hebbale · 18 November 2026

Quick answerThere is no single price. The cost of a small business website depends mainly on how many pages it has, what it needs to do (gallery, booking, chatbot), and who handles hosting and changes. The fair way to buy one is to ask for a fixed quote that lists exactly what is included.

What actually changes the price

  • Number of pages. A one-page site (about, services, gallery, contact) costs much less than a ten-page site with separate service pages.
  • Features. A photo gallery, a "Book a date" button, an enquiry form, WhatsApp chat, a blog or an AI chatbot each add work.
  • Content. If you already have good photos and text, it's faster. If the developer writes the text or edits images, that's extra time.
  • Custom design vs template. A design built around your brand takes longer than filling in a ready-made template, but it stands out.
  • Hosting, domain and changes. Ask who pays for the domain (yourbusiness.in) and hosting each year, and how small changes are handled after launch.

What every small business website should include

  • Looks good and loads fast on a phone, because most of your customers will open it on mobile.
  • Your phone number and a WhatsApp button on every screen.
  • Clear services, real photos of your work, and your location.
  • Basic Google setup (page titles, descriptions, Google Business link) so people can find you.

How to get a fair quote

  1. Write down what you want customers to do on the site: call, book, visit, or order.
  2. Collect 2–3 websites you like.
  3. Ask for a fixed price that lists the pages, features, hosting and how many rounds of changes are included.
  4. Ask to see a live preview before you pay the final amount.
