What an AI chatbot can (and can't) do for a small business

By Sachin Hebbale · 18 November 2026

Quick answerAn AI chatbot can answer common customer questions 24/7 from your own information (prices, timings, services), collect enquiries and send them to you. It cannot replace you for negotiations, complaints or anything it hasn't been given information about, so a good bot knows when to hand over to a human.

What a chatbot does well

  • Answers repeated questions instantly: "What are your timings?", "Do you deliver?", "How much is the pre-wedding package?"
  • Works at night. Customers often message late. The bot replies straight away instead of the lead going cold.
  • Collects enquiries. It can ask for the name, date and requirement, then send you a neat summary.
  • Speaks the customer's language when it's set up for it.

What it should not do

  • Make up answers. A well-built bot only answers from the information you give it. This is called grounding, and it's the most important part.
  • Handle complaints or price negotiation. Those need you.
  • Replace your personal touch. It handles the first reply so you can spend your time on serious customers.

Does your business need one?

You probably do if you answer the same 10 questions every day, you lose customers because you reply late, or you get enquiries outside working hours. You probably don't yet if you only get a few messages a week; a clear website with a WhatsApp button is enough.
