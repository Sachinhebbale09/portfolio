08 · Questions

quick answers

The questions clients ask me most, answered straight.

What does Sachin Hebbale do?

Sachin Hebbale is an AI Engineer and freelance web developer based in Belagavi, Karnataka. He builds websites for local businesses such as photographers, studios and shops, and adds AI chatbots and automation to them.

How much does a website cost in Belagavi?

It depends on the number of pages and features, for example a gallery, booking button or chatbot. Send Sachin a WhatsApp message with what you need and you get a clear, fixed quote, usually within a day, with no hidden costs.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size of the site. You do not wait until the end: you get a live preview link early, you ask for changes, and the site goes live once you are happy with it.

Do you work with clients outside Belagavi?

Yes. Sachin works with businesses in Belagavi, Hubballi-Dharwad and across Karnataka, and with clients anywhere in India remotely over WhatsApp and calls.

Can you add an AI chatbot to my existing website?

Yes. Sachin works professionally as an AI Engineer and can add a chatbot that answers customers from your own FAQs, prices and services, takes enquiries, and hands difficult questions to you.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. You explain your business and what you want; Sachin handles the design, building, hosting and launch. You only review a preview link on your phone.

Who hosts the website and can I use my own domain?

Sachin sets up the hosting and launch for you, and the site can run on your own domain name, for example yourbusiness.in.

Can you help my business show up on Google and in AI search?

Yes. Sachin does SEO (ranking on Google), local SEO for Google Maps and "near me" searches, GEO so your business appears in AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI answers, and AEO with FAQ sections Google can show as direct answers. He also writes blog posts and FAQ content for your website.

Can you make a logo animation for my brand?

Yes. Sachin creates cinematic logo reveals and intros for production houses, studios, creators and brands, delivered in Full HD with sound and in versions for reels and YouTube. See the Onset Media logo reveal in his work.
